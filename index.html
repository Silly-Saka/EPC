These practice exams recreate how HSC Enterprise Computing questions are presented and answered in the NESA online exam: question navigation, flagging, highlighting, font size and colour settings, the drawing tool, the spreadsheet tool, the SQL editor and written-response boxes with word counts.

The 2025 HSC and the familiarisation questions are the NESA online versions. The trial and preliminary papers were converted from paper-based exams into the online format.

When you finish (Exit exam), you can review every question with its marking guidelines, see your mark for multiple-choice and other auto-marked items, and self-mark written answers.

Your answers save automatically in this browser, so you can close the page and resume later.
